BEFORE THE ROAD TRIP

A few days in New York

I arrived in New York on September 19 and stayed at the Hilton Garden Inn on Whitehall Street, close to South Ferry. Before setting off on the road trip, I spent the evenings getting to know the city.

I took the Staten Island Ferry and saw the Statue of Liberty, had lunch at River Dock Cafe, and took my first subway ride to Times Square. Over the next few days I explored the High Line, SoHo and the World Trade Center area.

I also walked through NYU's Washington Square campus and Washington Square Park, where university life spills into Greenwich Village, and visited Union Square Park. These walks were a good introduction to navigating an unfamiliar city before the longer drive began.

THE TRAVEL JOURNAL

Day by day

DAY 1 · FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 25

Long Island, the ferry and Providence

We left Roslyn Harbour around 4:30 p.m. and drove to Port Jefferson for the 6:45 p.m. ferry to Bridgeport, Connecticut. Before boarding, we stopped at Tommy's Place for drinks and sides. The crossing took about an hour and fifteen minutes and was a relaxed way to begin the trip.

After landing, we continued along the Connecticut coast. Dinner was at Shell & Bones around 9 p.m., and we spent the night at a Holiday Inn Express near Smithfield, outside Providence.

DAY 2 · SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 26

Boston, Cambridge and Vermont

We reached Boston late in the morning and explored the harbour, East Boston and Boston Common, including the Soldiers and Sailors Monument. We also stopped at Fenway Park before heading to Harvard Square in Cambridge.

Lunch was ramen at Santouka, around 3:42 p.m. Later we drove north, paused at a Mexican bar in New Hampshire, and checked into Motel 6 in Colchester, Vermont.

DAY 3 · SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 27

Burlington, Smugglers' Notch and Lake George

We started in Burlington, passing the University of Vermont before spending time by Lake Champlain. We stopped for lunch and drinks at Spot on the Dock and saw a train arrive at Burlington station.

Rain meant we couldn't take the planned tour at Shelburne Farms, so we continued to Jeffersonville and El Gato Cantina. The drive through Smugglers' Notch and Stowe was a highlight. We reached Ben & Jerry's around 6:45 p.m., just before closing, then drove about two and a half hours to Lake George.

DAY 4 · MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28

A quiet morning at Lake George

We walked around Lake George, visited the Steamboat Company store and had breakfast and coffee at Café Vero. The café's miniature brewery was an unexpected discovery. We also visited Fort William Henry before leaving around 2:30 p.m.

We drove south and west through New York and Pennsylvania and spent the night at a Holiday Inn Express in Carlisle, Pennsylvania.

DAY 5 · TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 29

Gettysburg and Washington, DC

We left Carlisle around 10:30 a.m. and drove through Gettysburg National Military Park, including Little Round Top. The day then turned to Washington, DC, where we visited the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum, the World War II Memorial, Washington Monument and Lincoln Memorial.

In the evening we went to Georgetown for drinks at Sovereign and dinner at Reren, then visited George Mason University in Fairfax.

DAY 6 · WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30

Bristol, Knoxville and Nashville

We left around 10:45 a.m. and drove through the mountains towards Tennessee. In Bristol, we stopped at Legit's Kitchen for soul food and visited the Birthplace of Country Music Museum. In Knoxville's Old City, we took a coffee break at Awaken Coffee and stopped for a photo at the Virginia–Tennessee border.

We reached Nashville around 9:10 p.m. and missed the Bluebird Cafe show, which had already started. Instead, we walked around Lower Broadway and visited live-music bars including Robert's Western World and Posty's. We finally returned to the hotel around 2:30 a.m.

DAY 7 · THURSDAY, OCTOBER 1

The Ryman, Jack Daniel's and Memphis

We started late, leaving the hotel around 11 a.m., and visited the Ryman Auditorium for its history show and memorabilia. From there we drove to Lynchburg for the Jack Daniel's distillery tour, arriving around 1:45 p.m. We spent about three and a half hours there before continuing to Memphis, where we arrived around 11 p.m.

DAY 8 · FRIDAY, OCTOBER 2

Memphis, the Mississippi and the long drive to Texas

We left Memphis around 10:30 a.m. in heavy rain. We skipped the Graceland mansion tour because of the time and cost, but visited the gift store, Aretha Franklin's birthplace, Beale Street and the Bass Pro Pyramid. We also walked along Mud Island's Mississippi River trail, with its model showing the river's bends.

After leaving Memphis around 3:30 p.m., we stopped at the Old Mill in North Little Rock and Junction Bridge in Little Rock. We continued to Texarkana, where we visited the Arkansas–Texas state line near the US Post Office, had dinner at Verona Italian, and set off again around 10:30 p.m. We reached Houston at about 3:30 a.m. on October 3.

THE ROAD TRIP, IN RETROSPECT

Not everything went to plan.
That was part of the trip.

Rain changed our plans at Shelburne Farms, we missed the Bluebird Cafe show, and we skipped the Graceland mansion tour. But there were plenty of small discoveries too: a train arriving in Burlington, a last-minute ice-cream stop, live music on Lower Broadway and the unexpected Old Mill in Arkansas.

The drive was long, especially the final push into Houston, but the variety was what made it memorable: coastal towns, mountain roads, historic sites, music, food and the Mississippi River — all connected by the open road.
